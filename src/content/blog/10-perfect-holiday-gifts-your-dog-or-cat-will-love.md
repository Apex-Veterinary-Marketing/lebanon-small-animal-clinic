The holidays aren’t complete without wagging tails, happy purrs, and a few presents under the tree for your favorite companion. For many families, pets are part of every celebration, from movie nights to big family dinners, and it only feels right to include them in the gift-giving fun.

Still, finding the right pet gifts can be tricky. You want something special that makes your dog or cat feel loved but also supports their long-term health and happiness.  

Between festive toys, cozy gear, and safe holiday treats, the best gifts are the ones that bring joy and care together. 

At Lebanon Small Animal Clinic, our Care Companions love seeing pets enjoy the holidays safely and stress-free. We’ve put together a list of thoughtful, vet-approved Christmas gifts that are as practical as they are heartwarming. 

Read on for **10 ideas** to inspire your shopping list. Each one is designed to keep your pet feeling happy, healthy, and loved through the season and beyond.

## Holiday Gifts That Show Pets How Loved They Are

Every pet has their own personality, but all of them share one thing in common: they thrive when they feel secure, healthy, and cared for. Whether you’re shopping for a playful puppy, a senior cat, or a recently rescued friend celebrating their first holiday at home, these gifts combine comfort, enrichment, and wellness.

### 1. Nutritious Holiday Treats

Sharing seasonal snacks is part of the fun, but not everything on the table is safe for pets. [Choose vet-approved treats](/blog/holiday-foods-your-pets-can-enjoy/) in our recent blog post or try simple homemade recipes that skip harmful ingredients.  

Avoid chocolate, xylitol, grapes, and raisins, which can cause serious illness. Safe, healthy treats let pets join in on the festivities without risk, so everyone enjoys the season together.

### 2. Cozy Bedding or Blankets

Cold weather calls for extra comfort. A new bed or soft blanket provides warmth and joint support while creating a safe retreat during busy family gatherings. Pets, especially those who are anxious around guests or noise, appreciate having a calm spot that’s all their own.

### 3. Fun and Durable Toys

Toys keep the holidays lively and encourage healthy play. Chew toys, puzzle feeders, or feather wands help pets stay active while stimulating their minds. Engaging toys are also a wonderful way for families to bond with their pets during the slower winter days at home.

### 4. Stylish Collars and ID Tags

A festive new collar or ID tag adds a touch of style while keeping safety top of mind. Bright seasonal colors make pets stand out in holiday photos, but the real value is in updated identification. Check that your pet’s tags and microchip information are current, especially if guests will be coming and going from your home.

### 5. Grooming Essentials

Looking good and feeling good often goes hand in hand. Brushes, shampoos, nail trimmers, or even a professional grooming appointment make great gifts. Regular grooming keeps coats shiny, reduces shedding, and helps pets look their best for all those family photos.

### 6. Winter Gear for Pets

When temperatures drop, outdoor adventures require a little extra protection.  

Consider:

- Dog coats to maintain warmth
- Booties to shield paws from salt and ice
- Paw balms to soothe and protect dry skin

These small additions help make walks more comfortable and keep your pet safe from harsh conditions.

### 7. Subscription Boxes or Monthly Surprises

If you love giving gifts that keep the excitement going, subscription boxes are a fun choice. With toys, treats, and enrichment items delivered each month, your pet will look forward to every delivery. It’s a joyful reminder that care and play continue all year long.

### 8. Bonding Experiences

Sometimes the best gifts aren’t things, they’re moments. Spend extra time with your pet through training classes, enrichment activities, or long walks together. These shared experiences strengthen the bond between you and your companion, building trust and lifelong memories.

### 10. Don’t Forget Health and Wellness

The best gifts begin with good health. [Scheduling your pet’s annual exam](https://portal.chckup.app/book/lebanon-small-animal-clinic?booking_source=Other) or enrolling them in a PAWS Plan wellness membership give them a healthy start to the new year.
