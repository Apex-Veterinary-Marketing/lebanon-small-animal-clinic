## How PAWS Plans Help You Save

Preventive care is often one of the best investments you can make in your pet’s long-term health. Our team designed PAWS Plans to help families plan ahead, reduce financial stress, and avoid the higher costs that come with delayed treatment.

### Predictable Costs, Fewer Surprises

We designed PAWS Plans to help you manage preventive care through simple monthly payments. Instead of paying for each visit or service individually, you can count on:

- Monthly payments
- Bundled services that offer more value compared to one-time costs
- Fewer unexpected bills caused by delayed care or missed appointments

This approach simplifies planning and provides dependable pet wellness coverage throughout the year.

### Preventive Care Helps Avoid Bigger Problems Later

Routine care is one of the most effective ways to protect your pet’s long-term health. With consistent checkups and screenings, your veterinary team can:

- Detect issues early and reduce the need for advanced or emergency treatments
- Keep vaccines and parasite control up to date to help lower pet healthcare costs over time
- Maintain regular communication with you to guide timely, informed decisions about your pet’s care
