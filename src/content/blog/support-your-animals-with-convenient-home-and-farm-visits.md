Have you ever wished your herd could get the care they need without spending your day loading, hauling, and waiting? Mobile visits make that possible by bringing trusted support directly to your property.

When animals receive care at home, they’re more likely to stay steady and cooperative. There’s no travel, no unfamiliar noise, and no crowded waiting room. They stay in a place that feels familiar, which helps your veterinarian complete a thorough exam and give an accurate assessment.

At Lebanon Small Animal Clinic, our Care Companions bring reliable veterinary care to your barn, pasture, or stable.

In this article, you will see how home and farm visits help simplify your routine and ease stress for your animals.

## Home and Farm Visits Create a Calm and Efficient Care Experience

When your veterinarian provides care on-site, your routine stays intact, and your animals remain calm in the environment they know best.

Livestock can be examined and treated with less disruption, which lowers the chances of injury, and reduces anxiety. This helps your veterinarian gather a clear picture of their health.


### Reduced Stress for Every Animal

Familiar surroundings help livestock remain calm, especially during exams or vaccinations. Instead of reacting to unfamiliar sights and sounds, they stay grounded in their daily environment.

This leads to smoother handling and more accurate evaluations. It also makes it easier for you to support your animals, since you’re not working against the stress of transport.

Our Care Companions apply Fear Free techniques in every setting. That same commitment to gentle handling and animal comfort extends to barns, paddocks, and pastures.

### Efficient Care Without Transport Challenges

Trailering, loading, and hauling livestock can take a toll on both animals and owners. For some, the stress of transport leads to behavioral changes or physical strain that lingers long after the trip.

With **mobile veterinary care**, you avoid:

- The risk of injury or strain during loading
- The time spent coordinating transportation
- The stress animals experience in unfamiliar environments

Your vet arrives prepared and focused, so care happens without the disruption of leaving home.

### A Better Option for Large or Hard-to-Move Animals

Some animals need extra space or special handling that isn’t easily managed offsite. Moving them can be risky, time-consuming, or simply not practical based on their size or condition.

Our Care Companions bring:

- Calm, professional handling experience
- Equipment designed for on-site evaluations
- A respectful approach that considers your animals' safety and your setup

Whether you're managing pasture for dairy cattle, monitoring a pregnant doe, or keeping your flock settled, we provide real-time care in the environment that works best for you.


## Services That Support Livestock Health All Year

Planning ahead makes a real difference when it comes to keeping your animals productive and protected. Our mobile services help you stay on track with seasonal care and respond quickly to changes in health or behavior.

We offer farm visits for a variety of species, including goats, sheep, pigs, chickens, ducks, and cows. Each visit is tailored to your goals and designed to keep your animals healthy throughout the year.

### Vaccinations That Protect Herd Wellness

Vaccines help prevent contagious diseases that can spread through a group quickly. Staying current with your vaccination schedule lowers the risk of illness and helps maintain herd stability.

Our team helps you:

- Build a vaccination plan based on species and age
- Time seasonal boosters appropriately
- Reduce the likelihood of long-term health issues

Consistent updates reduce risk and help protect every animal under your care.

### Health Assessments That Catch Concerns Early

Subtle health changes can go unnoticed in a busy routine. Over time, those small signs may grow into larger concerns that affect the whole herd. Regular checkups help us catch things early, when treatment is often simpler and outcomes are better.

Our on-site health assessments can identify:

- Weight fluctuations or nutritional gaps
- Skin, coat, or hoof issues
- Signs of parasites or environmental strain
- Changes in behavior or reproductive status

These visits give you more clarity and help you make informed decisions about your animals’ well-being.
