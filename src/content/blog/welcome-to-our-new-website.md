Welcome to the newly redesigned Lebanon Small Animal Clinic website! We're excited to unveil our fresh look and user-friendly features.

For over 20 years, we’ve been proud to care for the pets—and people—of our community with the kind of personalized, compassionate service only a local team can offer.

From dogs and cats to farm animals and exotics, we treat all kinds of companions with expert care tailored to their unique needs. Our Fear Free trained team and cozy, calming space help pets feel at ease. With online booking, house calls, and same-day appointments, we make getting care simple and stress-free.

Explore our new site to learn about our full range of services, convenient PAWS wellness plans, and the community work we’re passionate about.
