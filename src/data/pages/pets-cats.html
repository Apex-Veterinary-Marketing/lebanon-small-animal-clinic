Whether your cat is a homebody or an adventurous spirit, they benefit from consistent, thoughtful veterinary care.

Our semi-annual wellness exams are designed to catch changes early and help your cat stay healthy through every stage of life. We evaluate weight, dental health, skin and coat, behavior, and more—creating a full picture of your cat’s well-being.

We’ll guide you through vaccinations based on your cat’s needs, including core protection like rabies and feline distemper (FVRCP), and lifestyle-based vaccines such as feline leukemia (FeLV).

We also provide routine parasite prevention to keep your cat comfortable and safe. With calm handling and personalized attention, our team makes each visit as stress-free as possible.
