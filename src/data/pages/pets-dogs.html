Routine care is the foundation of a long, happy life for your dog. From puppyhood through the golden years, our Care Companions offer comprehensive wellness exams, customized vaccination plans, and early detection for age-related changes. We focus on everything from skin and coat condition to joint health, dental care, behavior, and more.

We’ll help you stay up to date on core vaccines like rabies and distemper, as well as lifestyle-based protection like Bordetella or Lyme. Year-round flea, tick, and heartworm prevention is also part of your dog’s care plan. Every visit is a chance to check in, catch concerns early, and build trust with your pet—because your dog deserves more than a quick check-up.
