We’re proud to offer experienced care for a wide range of exotic species, including birds, lizards, snakes, turtles, hedgehogs, sugar gliders, ferrets, and more. Exotic pets have unique needs, and our team is trained in handling and treating species that require specialized knowledge and equipment.

Wellness exams help us monitor husbandry, nutrition, and environmental needs, as well as catch early signs of illness or stress. While some exotic pets may require vaccinations or routine parasite checks, most benefit most from preventive care and education. If you’re unsure what your exotic pet needs, we’re happy to help guide you.
