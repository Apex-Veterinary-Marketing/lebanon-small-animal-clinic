Guinea pigs, hamsters, rats, and other small companions need just as much attention as larger pets. Our clinic provides routine wellness exams to monitor weight, appetite, behavior, and dental health—key indicators of small animal well-being.

We help you navigate nutrition, enclosure setup, and signs of illness specific to pocket pets. While vaccines aren’t typically required, regular check-ins allow us to spot concerns early and support your pet’s long-term health. These tiny companions bring big joy, and we’re here to help you care for them every step of the way.
