Preparing for your first visit? We’re here to help make the experience smooth and stress-free—for both you and your pet.

When your pet or farm animal needs attention and leaving home isn’t an option, we’re here to help.

Lebanon Small Animal Clinic offers limited house call and farm visit services for urgent needs and routine care that’s better handled on-site. Whether it’s a health concern with a large animal or a situation that makes travel difficult, our team can bring trusted veterinary care directly to you.

We’re proud to offer mobile vet support — for farm animals and livestock—in the surrounding area. While we don’t operate a full-service vet van, we do provide flexible, location-based care when it fits the situation and patient best.

If your animal requires urgent attention or you’re unsure if a house call is right for your situation, give us a call. We’ll help determine the best next step for your animal’s care.
