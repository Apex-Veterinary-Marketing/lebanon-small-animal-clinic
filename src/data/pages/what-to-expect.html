Preparing for your first visit? We’re here to help make the experience smooth and stress-free—for both you and your pet.

If this is your first time with us, we’ll begin with a full wellness exam. This gives our team a complete picture of your pet’s health before moving forward with any treatments or recommendations. It’s a standard step we take to provide safe, informed, and personalized care.

To help us get started on the right foot, please bring:

  • Your pet’s most recent medical and vaccine records
  • A list of current medications or supplements
  • Any questions or concerns you’d like to discuss

You’re also welcome to bring your pet’s favorite blanket or toy to help them feel more at home.
