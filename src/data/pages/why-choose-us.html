Homegrown Care for Every Cluck and Meow

For over 20 years, we’ve been caring for the critters of our community. Along the way, we’ve become the neighborhood clinic loved by pets and trusted by families.

As a locally and privately owned clinic, we’ve built our care on the belief that every pet deserves the attention only a close-knit, community-based team can provide. Whether they bark, meow, bellow, baa, cluck, or crawl, we welcome pets of all shapes and sizes.

Our experienced team offers everything from routine checkups and dental cleanings to surgery and urgent visits for cats, dogs, small farm animals, and exotics alike.

We welcome them all, giving your one-of-a-kind companions the expert care they deserve, including but not limited to:

  • Cats
  • Dogs
  • Guinea pigs
  • Rabbits
  • Reptiles
  • Birds
  • Goats
  • Chickens

Support That’s Personal, Connected, and Convenient

We’ve designed every part of our clinic with your pet’s comfort in mind. Our team is trained in Fear Free practices and use a specially designed calming room to make visits as stress-free as possible.

Our team combines deep medical knowledge with ongoing training in the latest advancements. As USDA-accredited veterinarians, we’re proud to bring high-quality, advanced care to the place we call home.

Need flexibility? We offer online booking, same-day appointments, and house calls—because convenience matters just as much as quality care.

Proactive Wellness Plans for Every Life Stage

Preventive care is the best way to protect your pet’s health—and our PAWS Plans make it easy. These wellness memberships provide predictable, year-round care for your puppy, kitten, cat, or dog with services like exams, vaccines, bloodwork, and dental cleanings.

It’s everything you need to help your pet stay happy, healthy, and by your side for years to come.

Rooted in Community, Guided by Care

When you choose Lebanon Small Animal Clinic, you’re supporting a practice that’s deeply rooted in the community. We proudly give back to local 4-H Clubs, the Warren County Career Center, PAWS for Heroes, and animal rescue groups across the region.

Because caring for pets means caring for the people who love them most.
